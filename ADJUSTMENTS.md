# Waivers, bursaries, refunds, defaulters and cash-up (step 5)

## Waivers, bursaries and discounts
**Fees & payments -> Waivers & refunds -> + Waiver, bursary or discount.**
- Kinds: waiver, bursary (with the sponsor's name), discount, sibling discount, staff-child discount, other. A reason is required.
- A reduction can never be more than the student is charged. It covers their **oldest charges first**, then payments cover what is left.
- If the finance officer adds one, it waits for the principal on the **Approvals** page and only counts once approved. The principal's own entries count straight away.
- Statements show a **Waived** column and a "Reductions" list. Parents see the waived total and "Still to pay" on each charge.
- A student whose fees are fully waived counts as **Fees cleared**.
- Totals: **Charged** is what is owed after waivers; **Waived or reduced** is shown separately on the overview.

## Refunds
**Waivers & refunds -> + Refund.**
- A refund returns money a student paid **beyond what they owe** (a credit). It cannot be more than the credit, less refunds already waiting for approval.
- To refund a student who left, first reduce their remaining charges with a waiver. That creates the credit, and every step is on record.
- The finance officer asks and the principal approves. Refunds appear in the cash-up and the audit log.

## Defaulters and overdue ageing
**Defaulters tab:** who owes, how much is overdue, and for how long, with the parent's phone for follow-up. Filter by class, print a student's statement, or **download the list (CSV)**.
- A charge is late once its **due date** has passed. Charges with no due date show as "not yet due".
- Buckets: not yet due, 1 to 30, 31 to 60, 61 to 90, over 90 days late. A student's overdue amount sits in the bucket of their oldest unpaid charge.

## Cash-up
**Cash-up tab:** pick a day to see what was received, by method (cash, M-Pesa, bank, cheque), each receipt, any refunds, and payments reversed that day (not counted). **Print** gives a sheet to sign.

## Small things
- The class teacher's class results show the **Fees cleared** tag (no amounts).
- The principal's view-only screens no longer show edit buttons, and a refused change says so plainly.

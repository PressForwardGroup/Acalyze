# Fee structure (step 2)

## How a school sets up its fees
Open **Fees & payments -> Charges** (finance officer or principal).

Each charge has:
- **Name and amount**, and a **vote head** (Tuition, Boarding, Transport, Lunch...) for reports later.
- **Term** (or the whole year), a **due date**, and a **note for parents** (e.g. "Trip to Naivasha, pay by 20 Nov").
- **Grades that pay it**: tick the grades (for example Grade 1, 2 and 3). Leave all unticked for every grade.
- **Who pays it**: everyone, day scholars only, boarders only, or **chosen students only** (a transport route, lunch, a trip). For chosen students, use **Students** on the charge to tick who takes it.

Mark who boards with **Set boarders**, class by class. Everyone else is a day scholar.

## Drafts and publishing
- A new charge is a **draft**: it is not billed, and parents cannot see it.
- **Publish** makes the drafts count in balances and appear for parents. If the finance officer publishes, the principal approves first (Approvals page). The principal publishes directly.
- Only drafts can be edited. To change a published charge, delete it and add it again. A finance officer needs the principal's approval to delete a published charge.
- Charges that already existed before this update were published automatically, so nobody's balance changes.

## What a student owes
Every **published** charge that applies to them (their grade, day or boarding, and any optional charge they are assigned), minus what they have paid in the year.

## Fees cleared
A student is **cleared** when they have charges and have paid at least all of them for the year. School admins and deputies see only a **Fees cleared** tag next to the student in the Students list, never amounts. Students who are not cleared show no tag.

## Statements and "how to pay"
- Balances tab: **Statement** prints a student's fee statement: charges by term, payments, balance, and how to pay.
- **How parents pay**: text you write once (for example the Paybill and "account = admission number"). Parents see it on their fees page and on the statement.
- Parents see their child's published charges by term, with due dates and notes, and can print their statement.

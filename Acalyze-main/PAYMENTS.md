# Payments (step 3)

## Reversing instead of deleting
A payment is never deleted. **Reverse** (on the Record a payment tab) asks for a reason, then:
- the payment stays on record, shown struck through and marked **Reversed**, with who reversed it, when and why;
- it stops counting in balances, statements and reports, and its receipt prints with a **REVERSED** banner;
- the finance officer's request goes to the principal's **Approvals** page. The principal reverses directly.

## Safer recording
- If the student's balance is known, it is shown while you record a payment.
- Paying **more than the balance** asks you to confirm; the extra shows as a **credit**.
- Recording an **M-Pesa code that was already recorded** asks before accepting it again (usually a double entry).
- A payment cannot be dated in the future.
- The receipt shows who received the payment.

## Credits
A student who has paid more than they were charged has a **credit**, shown on their statement and in the overview ("Credits (overpaid)"). One student's credit never hides what another owes: **Outstanding** is the total of what students actually owe, and the **collection rate** counts only money that went towards charges.

## Oldest charges are paid first
Payments are not tied to one charge. A student's money is applied in order: whole-year charges, then Term 1, 2 and 3 (by due date, then name). So:
- a statement shows, for each charge, what was paid and what is still to pay;
- parents see "Still to pay" or **Paid** next to each charge;
- the fees overview shows **charged, paid and owed by vote head and by term**.

## Existing data
Nothing existing changes. Payments already recorded count as before. A payment that was deleted before this update is gone (it was deleted then). Approval requests to "void" that were still pending now reverse the payment when approved.
